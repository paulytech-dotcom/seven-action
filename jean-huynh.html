Bio

En quelquesIn a fewmotswords

Enfant, Jean grimpait et sautait partout. Le taekwondo, commencé à 15 ans, l’a mené jusqu’à la ceinture noire 5e dan et à fonder son propre club, H-Team, à Montpellier. Diplômé en STAPS, il a aussi été préparateur physique, sauveteur aquatique et moniteur de jet-ski : un bagage précieux quand il s’agit de sécurité.As a kid, Jean was always climbing and jumping. Taekwondo, which he started at 15, took him to a 5th Dan black belt and to founding his own club, H-Team, in Montpellier. With a sports science degree, he has also worked as a strength coach, lifeguard and jet-ski instructor: a valuable background when safety is at stake.

Depuis 2019, il est cascadeur professionnel : combats, chutes, acrobaties et cascades aquatiques sur des productions françaises et internationales. Dès son premier tournage, Jack Ryan, il intègre l’équipe qui conçoit les chorégraphies. Suivront notamment The Old Guard 2, Sous la Seine et K.O.A professional stunt performer since 2019, he works on fights, falls, acrobatics and water stunts for French and international productions. On his very first shoot, Jack Ryan, he joined the team designing the fight choreography. Credits since include The Old Guard 2, Under Paris and K.O.

Basé à Montpellier, il tourne entre Paris et l’étranger.Based in Montpellier, he works between Paris and abroad.
